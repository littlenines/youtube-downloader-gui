One-time setup

YT Downloader needs two free tools before it can download anything:

  • yt-dlp finds the video or song and downloads it.
  • ffmpeg converts audio to MP3 and joins picture and sound.

They are downloaded once (about 100 MB) into the app's own tools folder. Nothing is installed on your system.

Everything is installed. You can use the app now: paste a link to get started.
